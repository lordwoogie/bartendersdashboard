Our Story

From a love of beer and a love for our community.

Lively Beerworks began in 2018, when two veterans of the Oklahoma City beer scene decided to build the brewery they'd always wanted to work at. Between them they brought the better part of two decades in the industry — years spent brewing, selling, and learning how the right brewery becomes a gathering place.

Patrick Lively, an OKC native who came to beer after stints in politics and teaching, spent nine years in the industry before branching out on his own; he partnered up to turn a shared love of the craft into something permanent. The taproom opened downtown at the end of 2019. From the start, the idea was to put down roots — in the city, in the community, and in the beer itself.

The goal then is the goal now: honest, crushable beer and a friendly space that feels like home.
